# 12. Production and Technical Constraints

## Art Constraints
- No open world.
- No large explorable environments.
- No dependency on cinematic cutscenes.
- Limited unique enemy rigs.
- Reusable animation sets.
- Strong silhouettes and modular VFX.

## Technical Constraints
- Mobile-first.
- Fixed or constrained camera preferred.
- Small number of simultaneous combatants.
- Compact arenas.
- Data-driven upgrades and enemies.
- Modular content.

## Proposed MVP
- One playable character.
- One weapon.
- One arena.
- Three enemy types.
- One boss.
- Approximately ten upgrades.
- One complete run structure.
