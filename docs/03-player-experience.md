# 3. Player Experience

## Intended Player Fantasy
The player is a capable but vulnerable warrior surviving increasingly dangerous fantasy encounters, adapting their fighting style through temporary upgrades and gradually becoming powerful enough to overcome previously impossible opponents.

## Target Feelings
- Tension before an enemy attack.
- Satisfaction from a correctly timed block, parry, dodge, or counter.
- Excitement when a build starts to synergize.
- Pride after surviving a difficult encounter with little margin for error.
- Curiosity about what a different run could become.

## Desired Player Statements
- “I learned that enemy's pattern.”
- “That parry was perfect.”
- “My build became ridiculous.”
- “I want to try a different weapon/build next run.”

## Anti-Goals
The player should not feel that success depends primarily on:
- Memorizing opaque systems.
- Grinding unavoidable numerical advantages.
- Watching long unskippable sequences.
- Losing because of unreadable attacks or random unavoidable damage.
