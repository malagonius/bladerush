# 1. High Concept

## Working Title
BladeRush

## One-Sentence Pitch
A mobile-first action roguelite inspired by the readable, arcade-like presentation of Crossed Swords and the character scaling/build progression of Infinity Blade.

## Expanded Concept
The player fights through sequential fantasy encounters using timing-based, directional melee combat. Victories provide temporary run rewards that shape a build, while persistent progression unlocks broader options and long-term growth. The game prioritizes satisfying combat, readable enemy behavior, short mobile sessions, and a production scope compatible with a small team or solo developer.

## Inspirations
- Crossed Swords: presentation, arcade fantasy identity, enemy approach, manageable encounter structure.
- Infinity Blade: cinematic readability, mobile controls, equipment/stat scaling, escalating enemies, strong 1v1 combat focus.
- Roguelite genre: randomized rewards, run variety, build experimentation, meta-progression.

## Explicit Non-Goals
- A literal remake or port of Crossed Swords.
- A recreation of Infinity Blade's exact IP, characters, assets, or story.
- AAA-quality art or cinematic production.
- Open-world exploration.
