# 11. World and Content Structure

## Content Philosophy
Prefer handcrafted modular encounters assembled into varied runs rather than fully procedural worlds.

## Potential Content Units
- Combat encounter.
- Elite encounter.
- Event.
- Shop.
- Healing/rest point.
- Challenge room.
- Boss.
