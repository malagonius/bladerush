# 6. Camera and Presentation

## Direction
The visual target is closer to Crossed Swords than to a fully free 3D action game: a constrained, readable combat space with enemies approaching the player and a strong sense of depth.

## Production Benefits
- Fixed or semi-fixed camera.
- Small arenas.
- Reusable backgrounds.
- Limited animation requirements.
- Strong silhouette-based enemy readability.
- Effects and lighting can provide polish without huge asset counts.

## Candidate Models
### A — 2.5D Arcade
Mostly fixed player position, enemies approach along depth lanes.

### B — Fixed 3D Arena
Player and enemy occupy a compact arena with cinematic camera framing.

### C — Hybrid
Crossed Swords-like staging with Infinity Blade-like interaction and modern effects.

## Current Recommendation
Explore option C first.
