# 10. Difficulty and Progression

## Player Power
Potential dimensions:
- Damage.
- Survivability.
- Weapon strength.
- Defensive effectiveness.
- Abilities.
- Build synergy.

## Enemy Power
Difficulty may increase through:
- Health and damage.
- Faster or more complex patterns.
- New attack types.
- Resistances.
- Elite modifiers.
- Boss mechanics.

## Principle
Difficulty should not rely exclusively on inflated enemy health pools. Better enemy behavior and new combat problems are preferred.
