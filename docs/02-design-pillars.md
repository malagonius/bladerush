# 2. Design Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Combat First
The moment-to-moment combat must be enjoyable before adding content, progression, lore, or complexity.

## Pillar 2 — Readable Over Beautiful
Clear silhouettes, attack telegraphs, hit feedback, and understandable states matter more than visual fidelity.

## Pillar 3 — Every Run Creates a Build
Temporary choices should meaningfully alter the player's combat approach rather than only increasing generic numbers.

## Pillar 4 — Mobile-Friendly Sessions
Controls, encounter length, UI, and pacing must work on a touchscreen and support short sessions.

## Pillar 5 — Production-Conscious Design
Features must be selected with the capabilities of a small/solo developer in mind. Reusable assets, fixed cameras, modular enemies, and data-driven content are preferred.

## Pillar 6 — Learnable, Not Randomly Unfair
Failure should usually be understandable: the player missed a timing, misunderstood an enemy, or made a risky build decision.
